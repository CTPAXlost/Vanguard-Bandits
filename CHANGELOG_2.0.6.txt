VANGUARD BANDITS REMASTER 2.0.6 — FULL-BODY ATAC + COLOURFUL 2D COMBAT FX

Goal
Keep the remastered PS1 silhouettes, stop floating/cut skeletal parts on the tactical
map, unify ATAC height, and make melee / magic attacks feel punchy without a 3D arena.

Changes
1. Tactical rendering
- AtacFactory.create_atac(..., "tactical") always builds MultiViewAtac full-body sheets.
- Segmented Skeleton3D skins remain available via render_context "skeletal" for tooling/smoke.
- No 3D duel/arena presentation for normal campaign combat.

2. Height normalization
- MultiViewAtac measures the opaque content of the front sheet and scales every ATAC
  toward a shared tactical height (~1.68 world units).
- battle_prototype applies recommended_tactical_scale for all units (allies and enemies).

3. Combat animation
- Richer set_combat_pose for slash / lunge / strong_slash / magic / hit reactions.
- New CombatFx helper: slash ribbons, thrust streaks, impact bursts, magic orbs, hit flash.
- Basic attacks and strong slash now drive pose curves plus colourful FX on the tactical map.
- CinematicVfx falls back to procedural CombatFx when a storyboard pack is missing.

4. Compatibility
- Afterimages correctly find CameraFacingRoot/ModelRoot/AtacSprite.
- Visibility smoke expects full-body tactical ATAC and still validates skeletal packs separately.
- Mission 5 smoke checks normalized scale for every ATAC visual.

5. CI fix
- tools/validate_project.py updated from 2.0.5 markers to 2.0.6 and the new tactical factory rules.

Version
- project.godot: 2.0.6
- Windows file/product version: 2.0.6.0
